# Evidence checklist per dimension

*v0.1.0, 2026-11-08. Ask for these before or during the assessment. For S, most evidence is a
conversation plus a screen-share; for L/XL, ask for documents and sample data. Record each item as
seen (document or system observed), stated (interview only) or not available. A score backed only
by "stated" evidence gets medium confidence at best.*

Handle client documents under the engagement's data-handling agreement only; never paste them into
public AI tools (conventions s2).

## Strategy and leadership
- [ ] Business plan or strategy mentioning AI, automation or digital priorities
- [ ] Named executive sponsor (email, org chart, board minute)
- [ ] Budget line or approval for AI or automation work
- [ ] List of AI ideas or initiatives, with any value estimates and who made them
- [ ] Results of any past pilots (including failed ones and why)

## Data
- [ ] List of systems of record and what each holds
- [ ] Data owners named per key entity
- [ ] Sample records or documents for the top candidate use cases (5-10 real examples)
- [ ] Data quality evidence: duplicate rates, report disputes, manual clean-up time
- [ ] Data classification scheme, if any
- [ ] Data sampling (L/XL): a structured sample pulled and checked for completeness and consistency

## Technology and infrastructure
- [ ] System inventory with versions, hosting and API availability
- [ ] Identity provider and SSO coverage
- [ ] Existing integration or workflow tools (Power Automate, Zapier, n8n, iPaaS)
- [ ] Cloud agreements and regions in use
- [ ] Availability of sandbox or test environments
- [ ] Any AI tools licensed (enterprise tenancy or individual consumer accounts)

## People and skills
- [ ] Org chart with IT, data and process-owner roles
- [ ] Training records or plans relating to digital or AI skills
- [ ] Short staff pulse (L/XL; 5-8 questions on current AI use, confidence and concerns)
- [ ] Examples of staff already experimenting
- [ ] History of the last two technology rollouts (what went well, what did not)
- [ ] Consultation obligations (enterprise agreement, union) that affect change

## Process
- [ ] Written procedures for the candidate processes
- [ ] Volumes and handling times (system reports, or a one-week sample the client runs)
- [ ] Exception lists or examples
- [ ] Process KPIs, if any

## Governance and risk
- [ ] AI policy or acceptable use policy (draft or published)
- [ ] Named accountable person for AI
- [ ] AI register or inventory of AI tools and use cases
- [ ] Risk register entries for AI; any impact or privacy impact assessments
- [ ] Current privacy policy (check for automated decision-making disclosure, APP 1.7 from 10 Dec 2026)
- [ ] Inventory of systems that make or shape decisions about people (ADM candidates), including vendor-embedded ones
- [ ] Sector obligations list (APRA, ASIC, TGA, AHPRA, government policy) where applicable
- [ ] Incident process covering AI

## Security and privacy
- [ ] MFA and SSO coverage on core systems
- [ ] Data handling rules for AI tools (which data may go to which tool)
- [ ] Evidence of shadow AI (expense data, browser extension audit, SSE or CASB logs at L/XL)
- [ ] Vendor terms for AI tools in use: training on customer data, residency, sub-processors, exit
- [ ] Notifiable data breach process
- [ ] Essential Eight maturity self-assessment or target (if the client uses it)
- [ ] Security questionnaire the client sends its own suppliers (shows their bar)
