# ai-discovery-scoping

Every value claim traces to the client's own numbers; missing numbers become follow-up questions, never estimates. Covers S to XL organisations in any industry.

**When to use:** Use when running discovery for an AI engagement and writing the scope of work -- preparing a first call, choosing the questions to ask, running a workshop or stakeholder interviews, qualifying and ranking candidate use cases, picking a pilot, matching a use case to a solution pattern, writing the solution design brief, or turning discovery answers into a scope of work with line items and three-point hours ready for ai-proposal-pricing to quote.

**Trigger phrases:** AI discovery, discovery call prep, first call with AI prospect, what questions should I ask, AI workshop agenda, stakeholder interviews for AI, qualify AI use cases, pick a pilot, AI solution design, solution design brief, scope the AI project, scope of work for AI, AI requirements, which AI pattern...
